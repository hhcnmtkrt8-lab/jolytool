The company

A fibrous materials factory built around one production line after another.

Gaopeng Building Materials Co., Ltd. was established in 2004, it lies in the city of Nantong in Jiangsu province, it's very close to the port of Shanghai. We are a professional manufactory for fiberglass self-adhesive tape, alkali-resistant fiberglass mesh, metal corner tape, paper tape, wall patch, etc.

We have been ISO 9001 certified for more than 10 years and with 20+ years' experience of production, and we always keep our strict quality control system to produce all of our products. We sell our products to many countries. 80% of our products are exported to the famous companies.

Our good quality products with reasonable prices will make your business to win more markets.

Manufacturing

What we control in-house

01

Weaving and coating

C-glass or E-glass yarn is woven into fabric, then coated with acrylic copolymer liquid and cured at high temperature.

02

Converting and slitting

Mesh is coated with self-adhesive latex and slit into roll widths and lengths matched to each market's packing standards.

03

Export packing

Cartons, roll counts and container loading plans are prepared for 20GP and 40HQ shipments.
