Fiberglass adhesive mesh

self-adhesive fiberglass meshreinforcing meshdrywall repair mesh

Fiberglass self-adhesive mesh provides reinforcement for both exterior and interior structures. It is used on stucco, cement, plastics, bitumen, marble, roofing and interior and exterior wall applications, and is widely used as an ideal reinforcing product.

It has following advantages

  • Excellent alkali resistance.
  • Fireproof.
  • Durable and corrosion resistant.
  • High-strength material.
  • Reinforcement for exterior and interior structures.
  • Suitable for stucco, cement, plastics, bitumen, marble and roofing applications.
  • Repairs drywall and plasterboard, restores damaged and recurring cracks.
  • Ideal and necessary material for interior wall repair and decoration in modern buildings.
  • Self-adhesive and easy to use.
