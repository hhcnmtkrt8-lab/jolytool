Fiberglass tissue tape

fiberglass mat tapetissue tape

Fiberglass tissue tape is made from fiberglass mat, it is mainly used as substrates for waterproof roofing materials and joint materials. It is characterized by high tensile strength, corrosion resistance, water resistance, fireproof, high porosity and easy application by bitumen and joint compound.
